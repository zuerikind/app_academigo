## Becoming a teacher
86. **How do I become a teacher?** Sign up as a teacher, verify your email, build your profile (bio, subjects, languages, CV, motivation letter, payout details), and submit for admin review.
87. **What qualifications do teachers need?** Every teacher is reviewed and approved by the Academigo team before going live; a motivation letter is required and a CV can be uploaded. Specific minimum qualifications aren't fixed in the system — contact WhatsApp for details.
88. **Do I need to upload a CV?** A CV upload is supported (PDF/DOC/DOCX, up to 5 MB) and helps the review; a motivation letter (at least 100 characters) is required.
89. **How are teachers verified?** An admin reviews the full profile, motivation letter, and CV before approving the teacher to go live.
90. **How much do teachers earn?** Pay rises with level: roughly CHF 30 per lesson at Junior, CHF 45 at Academigo Teacher, and up to CHF 60 at Verified. For exact current rates, contact omid@academigo.xyz.
91. **What are the teacher levels?** Junior, Academigo Teacher, and Verified — higher levels mean higher pay and more visibility.
92. **How do I get promoted as a teacher?** Complete more lessons and keep your rating high, then email omid@academigo.xyz with subject "Level promotion"; the team reviews your profile.
93. **How many lessons do I need to reach the next level?** Requirements grow with completed lessons and rating, but the exact thresholds differ between our published FAQ and internal settings — please confirm with omid@academigo.xyz rather than rely on a single number.
94. **How and when do teachers get paid?** Earnings are credited after you mark a session complete; you request a payout and an admin processes it, paid via IBAN or TWINT.
95. **How long do payouts take?** A specific payout timeframe isn't defined in the system — please contact Academigo via WhatsApp or omid@academigo.xyz.
96. **How do teachers set availability?** Through a weekly schedule editor, with date/time blockers for exceptions.
97. **Which subjects are you recruiting teachers for?** STEM, languages, humanities, and creative subjects.
98. **Can I teach in person and online?** Yes — teachers can offer online, in person (with a location), or both.
