  <name>Checkpoint: Browser verification of all Phase 3 flows</name>

Complete Phase 3 implementation: teacher availability management, student booking calendar and request flow, Stripe credit purchase, teacher booking queue with confirm/decline (with optional alternative slot picker)/mark-complete, student bookings with inline review forms, teacher earnings page with payout requests, ICS calendar export.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Run `npm run dev` and verify each flow. Delete .next and restart dev server if you encounter 404s on new routes (known Turbopack caching issue — see memory note).

**Flow 1 — Teacher availability (AVAIL-01/02):**
1. Sign in as a teacher
2. Navigate to /[locale]/teacher/availability
3. Add a weekly range: Monday 14:00–18:00
4. Verify it appears in the list
5. Add a blocker date for next Monday
6. Verify it appears in the blockers list
7. Remove one range and verify it disappears

**Flow 2 — Student sees availability (AVAIL-03):**
1. Sign in as a student
2. Navigate to a teacher's profile (/student/teachers/[id])
3. Verify a monthly calendar section appears below the teacher info
4. Verify days with availability are highlighted (Monday should be highlighted if you added availability in Flow 1)
5. Click Monday — verify 15-minute slots appear (14:00, 14:15, 14:30... up to 17:10 since last slot must end by 18:00)

**Flow 3 — Full booking cycle (BOOK-01 through BOOK-09):**
1. As student: click a slot → add topic note → click "Request session"
2. Verify booking appears on /student/bookings as "Awaiting confirmation"
3. Sign in as teacher → navigate to /teacher/bookings
4. Verify the pending booking appears with student name, date/time, topic note
5. Click "Confirm" → enter a Zoom link → submit
6. Verify booking shows as confirmed on both teacher and student bookings pages
7. Student: verify meeting link is visible on their bookings page
8. Student: verify ICS download link works (downloads a .ics file)
9. Teacher: click "Mark complete"
10. Student: verify booking appears in "Past sessions" section
11. Student: verify "Leave a review" button appears on the completed booking

**Flow 3b — Decline with alternative slot (BOOK-04):**
1. As teacher: on a pending booking, click "Decline"
2. Verify the inline form expands showing a dropdown of your available slots for the next 7 days
3. Select an alternative slot from the dropdown and submit
4. Verify the booking is cancelled and proposed_alternative_start_time is recorded (check in Supabase Studio or verify the cancelled card shows "Alternative offered: [date/time]")
5. Also verify: clicking Decline and submitting WITHOUT selecting a slot (plain decline) works correctly

**Flow 4 — Reviews (REV-01 through REV-04):**
1. As student: click "Leave a review" on a completed booking
2. Verify inline form expands (no modal, no page navigation)
3. Select 4 stars + add a comment + submit
4. Verify button disappears after submission
5. Navigate to the teacher's profile (/student/teachers/[id])
6. Verify review appears with stars and comment
7. Verify average rating and review count are displayed
8. Navigate to /student/teachers (directory)
9. Verify teacher card shows star rating and review count

**Flow 5 — Stripe credits (PAY-01 through PAY-05):**
(Requires STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET, and stripe_price_id populated on credit_packages)
1. Sign in as a student
2. Navigate to /student/packages
3. Verify credit packages are displayed with CHF prices
4. Verify current credit balance is shown
5. Click "Buy" on Essentials Single — verify redirect to Stripe Checkout
6. Use Stripe test card (4242 4242 4242 4242, any future exp, any CVC)
7. Complete payment — verify redirect back to /student/packages?success=true
8. Verify success banner appears
9. Verify credit balance has increased by 1

Note: If Stripe credentials are not yet configured, verify the error handling: click "Buy" → verify graceful error display instead of crash.

**Flow 6 — Teacher earnings and payout (EARN-01 through EARN-05):**

10. Click "Mark processed"
11. Sign in as teacher → verify payout status shows "processed"
